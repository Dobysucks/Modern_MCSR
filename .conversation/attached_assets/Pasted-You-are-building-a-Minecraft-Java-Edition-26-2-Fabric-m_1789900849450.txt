PART 2 — OVERWORLD START TYPES
==================================================

Implement the following filtered start types:

1. Village
2. Shipwreck
3. Desert Temple
4. Ruined Portal
5. Buried Treasure

The filter must support enabling/disabling each start type.

The intended start structure should be positioned according to the documented Ranked-style coordinate/filter model.

Do NOT simply search for the nearest random structure.

The filter must identify the intended structure and verify that it satisfies all requirements.

--------------------------------------------------
VILLAGE
--------------------------------------------------

Requirements:

- Village must spawn within **7 chunks** of the intended spawn/start area.
- Village must contain a guaranteed blacksmith-type building.
- The start must provide at least:

  **7 iron**

  OR

  **4 iron + 3 diamonds**

- Count relevant iron available from:
  - chests
  - iron golem
  - other explicitly allowed starting resources

- Iron Golem behavior must be standardized so that it always drops:

  **4 iron**

  rather than allowing the normal 3–5 range.

- Food must be available through:
  - village chests
  - hay bales
  - other explicitly documented food sources

- A river biome must exist within:

  **6 chunks of the center of the village**

- Three usable lava pools must be guaranteed near the village.

OR:

- The blacksmith must contain:

  **8+ obsidian**

  allowing a blacksmith-based enter.

- Artificially guaranteed lava pools must be distinguishable from ordinary naturally generated lava pools in the filter implementation.

- Taiga villages must guarantee:

  **10+ obsidian**

  in the appropriate blacksmith/start configuration.

Implement the special blacksmith variants correctly:

- Weaponsmith
- Toolsmith
- Armorer

and account for biome-specific variants.

--------------------------------------------------
SHIPWRECK
--------------------------------------------------

Requirements:

- Shipwreck within:

  **4 chunks**

  of the intended start.

- At least:

  **7 iron**

  OR

  **4 iron + 3 diamonds**

- Food must be available in the appropriate chest.

- Suspicious stew must NOT produce poison if this mechanic is part of the filtered-start implementation.

- Shipwreck should not be surrounded by irrelevant blocking structures/entities that interfere with the intended start.

Specifically account for:

- buried treasures
- other shipwrecks
- irrelevant block entities
- dungeons

- Shipwreck chests should not normally be buried in unusable terrain.

- Two magma ravines must be guaranteed within:

  **10 chunks**

  of the shipwreck.

- Do not count filtered magma ravines generated in Frozen Ocean biomes if the documented filter excludes them.

--------------------------------------------------

  **5 chunks**

  **7 iron**

  OR

  **4 iron + 3 diamonds**

- Food must be obtainable from the temple/start area.

- Nearby wood must be available.

- River biome within:

  **6 chunks**

  of the temple.

- Three usable lava pools must be guaranteed near the temple.

--------------------------------------------------
RUINED PORTAL
--------------------------------------------------

Requirements:

- Ruined Portal within:

  **3 chunks**

- At least:

  **18 iron nuggets**

  from the intended chest/resource setup, excluding resources required for:

  - bucket

- The filter must recognize that some ruined portals may not provide enough iron for an iron pickaxe.

- Food must be available from:
  - chest

- A reliable light/portal ignition method must exist.

Examples:

- Flint and Steel

- The Nether enter must be possible through:

  **Obsidian**

  or

  **Bucket**

  **80/20**

where applicable.

--------------------------------------------------
BURIED TREASURE

Requirements:

  **5 chunks**

  **7 iron**

PART 3 — NETHER FILTER
==================================================

The Nether filter is extremely important.

Do NOT merely require:

"bastion nearby + fortress nearby."

Implement the actual intended-bastion concept.

--------------------------------------------------
BASTION
--------------------------------------------------

The intended bastion must satisfy:

- It is the intended closest/useful bastion according to the filter algorithm.
- It must be at least:

  **10 chunks closer**

  than competing bastions according to the documented coordinate/distance calculation.

- Intended bastion must be within:

  **14 chunks of Nether 0,0**

- Perform an open-terrain check between the approximate Nether entry point and bastion.

- Bastion loot must contain:

  **3+ iron**

  AND

  **5+ obsidian**

  somewhere in the appropriate rampart chest configuration.

- Housing bastions may use the appropriate chest set rather than only rampart chests.

--------------------------------------------------
STABLES BASTION
--------------------------------------------------

For Stables bastions:

Require either:

- **2 good gaps**

OR

- **1 good gap + at least one triple-chest rampart**

Define "good gap" explicitly in code.

Do NOT implement it as a vague terrain check.

Create a dedicated:

BastionGapAnalyzer

class.

--------------------------------------------------
FORTRESS
--------------------------------------------------

The intended fortress must satisfy:

- Fortress origin within:

  **16 chunks**

  of the intended bastion.

- Open-terrain/pathability check between bastion and fortress.

Do not simply use Euclidean distance.

Implement the same chunk/region-based interpretation used by the filter specification.
